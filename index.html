重疾险赔款计算器Critical illness payout calculator

先看怎么算How to work it out

这张表帮你估算生重病后需要准备多少钱。你只要填数字，网页会帮你算。 Estimate how much money you may need after a serious illness. Enter your figures and the page does the maths.

  1. 1. 算生活费还差多少Work out the gap in living expenses

    每月必要开销减去每月还能用的收入，再乘以要准备的月数。 Subtract available monthly income from essential monthly expenses, then multiply by the number of months.

    仅为假设Example only
    每月必要开销Essential monthly expenses
    RM 4,000
    每月还能用的收入Monthly income still available
    − RM 1,000
    每月还差多少Monthly shortfall
    = RM 3,000
    要准备的月数Months to plan for
    × 24 个月24 months
    这段时间的生活费缺口Living expense gap for this period
    = RM 72,000

  2. 2. 算总共需要多少Work out the total payout needed

    用第 1 步算出的金额，加上自付医疗和康复费，再减去打算用的存款。 Take the amount from step 1, add medical and recovery costs you pay yourself, then subtract the savings you plan to use.

    仅为假设Example only
    第 1 步的生活费缺口Living expense gap from step 1
    RM 72,000
    自付医疗和康复费Medical and recovery costs you pay
    + RM 30,000
    打算用的存款Savings you plan to use
    − RM 20,000
    总共需要的赔款Total payout needed
    = RM 82,000

  3. 3. 算最后还差多少Work out how much more you need

    从总共需要的金额里，减掉现有重疾险能赔的钱。 Subtract what your existing critical illness cover would pay from the total payout needed.

    仅为假设Example only
    第 2 步算出的总金额Total payout needed from step 2
    RM 82,000
    现有重疾险能赔的钱Payout from existing critical illness cover
    − RM 50,000
    最后还差的赔款Additional payout needed
    = RM 32,000
